# Mission: Learn to Learn

## Why
I want to master the general skill of *learning how to learn* so I can apply it across everything I do — not just one domain. The framework I'm grounding this in is Dr. Justin Sung's evidence-based approach (iCanStudy): principles over tactics, building a personal learning system, and studying at the right *level of thinking*.

## Goals
- Understand the principles of efficient learning (vs. memorising tactics).
- Build a personal learning system I can apply to any subject.
- Learn faster and retain more, with less wasted time.

## Domains (where I'll apply this)
- Learning to code / front-end development faster (I freelance)
- Passing formal exams & certifications
- General professional upskilling & career growth
- General lifelong learning & self-improvement

## Success looks like
I can take any new subject and, using a repeatable system, reach a high level of competence in roughly half the time it used to take — and remember it.

## Constraints / preferences
- Process one transcript at a time; produce reference doc(s) from each, no repetition.
- Don't read all transcripts at once.
