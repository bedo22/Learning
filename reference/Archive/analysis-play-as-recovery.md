## What it answers
- What play is and is not: Brown's definition + 7 properties, the graded-not-checklist reading (Zosh 2018; Weisberg 2013 core criteria), "purposeless ≠ meaningless", play-vs-relaxation/escape/reward table, play-as-state-not-activity.
- When you need play: the recovery triage (signal→match→validate), play-specific signal list, sleep-vs-play tie-break via the 5-minute liking test, plus the rest-palette callout (body/mind/heart/hands/spirit meters).
- Why it works: four pillars (effort-recovery, wanting≠liking, boredom-as-exploration, broaden-and-build) plus the mastery pillar, flow-vs-play and DP-vs-play contrasts, and a five-line proof that play needs no prior mastery.
- How to make it happen: the play paradox (4 mechanical reasons planning fails), reclaiming-play steps for adults (Proyer/Brauer/Lubbers evidence), containers/menus/triggers with written-out if-then examples, proactive-vs-reactive recovery.
- When to stop: liking-satiation stop rule, external stops for wanting-feeds, the meaning trap (play slot vs mastery slot), persistent-boredom retriage, anhedonia referral.
- Honest limits: a dedicated research-disputes callout (forced play, wrong-system days, rumination risk, difficulty fit, Sutton-Smith's ambiguity warning) and a fully transparent self-admitted gap in the strict-list scoring method.
