## What the doc(s) claim from this source

- play-as-recovery claims: lunchtime park walks & relaxation exercises enhance daily well-being (Sianoja et al., 2018) — cited in the breaks/rest cluster.
- rest-and-recovery claims: nature (park) breaks recover — cited as nature-recovery evidence.

## Key findings (one line each, with supporting quote)

- **Park walks at lunch improve daily well-being** — an intervention (park walk vs relaxation exercise vs control) raised daily well-being.
- **Nature exposure during a break is a recovery intervention** — the park-walk condition shows nature + break combined recovers.

## Key quotes (with locations)

> "lunchtime park walks and relaxation exercises" — title (intervention conditions)

## Notes / caveats

- Randomized intervention (stronger than diary studies) but small scale; effect sizes modest.
- The doc uses it as nature-recovery evidence; the mechanism is Attention Restoration (Kaplan) + break recovery.
- **Does NOT support:** that play (Brown-style) is the mechanism — this is nature + relaxation, a rest-channel recovery.
