## Key findings (one line each, with supporting quote)

- **Forced play backfires** — when play/gamification is mandatory, its benefits drop or reverse (consent matters).
- **Consent is the boundary of play** — the doc's "the moment it's forced it stops being play" (Voluntary test) is empirically supported here.

## Notes / caveats

- SSRN preprint / conference proceedings — not peer-reviewed journal; the doc cites it as the consent counterargument, appropriately.
- The doc pairs it with Waytz's HBR piece; both support the "no forced play" rule.
- **Does NOT support:** that gamification never works — the point is *mandatory* gamification fails; voluntary gamified play can work.
