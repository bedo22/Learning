## What the doc(s) claim from this source

- play-as-recovery claims: "One more rule from the recovery paradox (named by Sonnentag, 2018)" — when you most need recovery (high stress), you're least able to achieve it (the day's stress bleeds into evening).
- play-as-recovery claims: the paradox explains why depleted evenings pick non-recovering activities (wanting-feed, numbing).

## Key findings (one line each, with supporting quote)

- **High job stressors predict poorer recovery — the paradox** — the people who most need recovery get it least; stressors impair the recovery process.
- **The paradox operates through impaired unwinding** — stress interferes with detachment/relaxation in the evening.

## Key quotes (with locations)

> "the recovery paradox" — Sonnentag's term for the finding that high stressors both increase recovery need and undermine recovery achievement
