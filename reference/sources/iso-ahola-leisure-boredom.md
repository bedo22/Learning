## Key findings (one line each, with supporting quote)

- **Passive leisure can become boredom/apathy, not recovery** — leisure boredom is a real state; unstructured passive time isn't automatically restorative.
- **Leisure boredom is conceptually measurable** — Iso-Ahola & Weissinger's scale/construct frames it.

## Notes / caveats

- The 1997 chapter is a broad leisure-and-health analysis; the doc's specific claim (passive → boredom) draws on the leisure-boredom construct.
- The DOI is the 1990 leisure-boredom article (Iso-Ahola & Weissinger), the empirical anchor for the claim.
- **Does NOT support:** that all leisure is boring; it's the "doing nothing ≠ recovery" caveat the doc makes.
