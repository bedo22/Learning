## What the doc(s) claim from this source

## Key findings (one line each, with supporting quote)

- **Immersion is the mechanism by which gaming recovers** — players curate immersion to escape work pressure (the doc's gaming-recovery note).
- **Recovery through gaming is deliberate** — players choose games/conditions to create immersion for recovery.

## Notes / caveats

- Qualitative/design-research framing (CHI HCI venue) — mechanism insight more than causal effect.
- Complements Koçak's quantitative mastery finding: immersion is the *experience* route, mastery is the *outcome* route.
- **Does NOT support:** that immersion alone recovers — the doc pairs it with Koçak's mastery-channel evidence.
