## Notes / caveats

- Longitudinal diary design — correlational within-person; energy and break-experience co-vary.
- The doc cites it in the breaks-restore-resources cluster; the mechanism (recovery experiences during breaks) is the shared thread with Hunter & Wu.
- **Does NOT support:** that lunch breaks *guarantee* recovery, or play-specific claims — it's about break experiences generally.
