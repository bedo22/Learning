## What the doc(s) claim from this source

- rest-and-recovery claims: Attention Restoration Theory / soft fascination (Kaplan) — nature restores directed attention through soft fascination.

## Key findings (one line each, with supporting quote)

- **Directed attention fatigues; nature restores it** — ART: directed attention depletes, and restorative environments (nature) allow it to replenish.
- **Soft fascination is the mechanism** — nature's effortless fascination (vs hard fascination) lets attention recover without effort.

## Notes / caveats

- ART is a theory (widely supported); the doc uses it for the nature-recovery channel.
- The doc links ART from the Wikipedia page; this digest anchors the primary source.
- **Does NOT support:** that play (engagement) restores via ART — ART is rest-channel (attention restoration), distinct from play's engagement channel.
