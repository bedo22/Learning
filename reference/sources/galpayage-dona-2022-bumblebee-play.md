## Key findings (one line each, with supporting quote)

- **Bumblebees roll balls repeatedly — a play-like behavior** — the study documented repeated, spontaneous ball-rolling without apparent purpose.
- **Burghardt's criteria applied to a non-mammal** — the study tested the behavior against Burghardt's five criteria, extending play's range.

## Notes / caveats

- The play interpretation is debated (some argue ball-rolling has other functions); the doc uses it as evidence that play criteria are graded/applied, not as settled fact.
- The doc's Burghardt digest notes this study applies all five criteria as tests.
- **Does NOT support:** that insects recover from work — it's play *recognition* across species, not recovery science.
