- **Play has been explained seven different ways historically** — the seven rhetorics (progress, fate, power, identity, imaginary, self, frivolity) are distinct interpretive traditions.
- **No single rhetoric wins** — play's ambiguity means any one explanation (including recovery) is a lens, not the truth; the doc's guardrail.
- **This is why "what is play" resists one definition** — the doc's honest-counterarguments section uses Sutton-Smith as the ambiguity counter.
