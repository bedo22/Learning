## What the doc(s) claim from this source

- rest-and-recovery claims: "Sonnentag & Fritz's four experiences cover *energy and control*. The DRAMMA model (Newman et al., 2014) extends them with two further needs — **affiliation** and **meaning**."
- play-as-recovery claims (cite): Newman, Tay & Diener, DRAMMA model.

## Key findings (one line each, with supporting quote)

- **DRAMMA extends recovery to six mechanisms** — Detachment, Relaxation, Autonomy, Mastery, Meaning, Affiliation — adding meaning + affiliation to the REQ's four.
- **Affiliation and meaning are separate needs** — socializing (affiliation) and purpose (meaning) meet needs the REQ's four don't cover.

## Key quotes (with locations)

> DRAMMA — the six mechanisms: detachment, relaxation, autonomy, mastery, meaning, affiliation (model, J. Happiness Studies, 2014)

## Notes / caveats

- A model/synthesis paper integrating leisure-and-well-being research; the mechanisms are proposed mediators.
- The doc's "affiliation gets confused with rest" point is a faithful application: affiliation is a *separate* need from cognitive rest.
- **Does NOT support:** that affiliation = cognitive recovery; the doc explicitly distinguishes them.
