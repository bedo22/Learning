## What the doc(s) claim from this source

- wanting-vs-liking claims (history table, "≤1950s / Drive-reduction theory (Hull)"): "Motivation = reducing biological drives; pleasure = the relief of that reduction."

## Key findings (one line each, with supporting quote)

- **Motivation was cast as drive reduction** — behavior is directed at reducing biological drives (hunger, thirst); this was the pre-1980s template for "why we act."
- **Pleasure was identified with the relief of drive reduction** — the idea the wanting/liking arc explicitly overturned (the doc's "dopamine ≠ pleasure" history begins here).
