## Key findings (one line each, with supporting quote)

- **Metacognition is knowledge about cognition plus monitoring of it** — the field of studying how people think about, monitor, and control their own cognitive processes.
- **Four types of metacognitive knowledge** — knowledge about persons (your own traits), tasks (difficulty demands), strategies (what works), and interactions between these.
- **Cognitive monitoring is the active process** — people continuously monitor their comprehension, learning, and memory, and this monitoring drives strategy selection.

## Key quotes (with locations)

> "Metacognition refers to one's knowledge concerning one's own cognitive processes and products or anything related to them." — the foundational definition

> "Metacognition plays a crucial role in the regulation of cognition." — on the monitoring-control link

## Notes / caveats

- This is a theoretical/narrative paper — Flavell was laying out a research agenda, not reporting empirical findings.
- The concept was rapidly adopted and has become one of the most influential frameworks in cognitive and educational psychology.
- **Does NOT support:** that metacognition is a single skill that can be trained in isolation — it interacts with domain knowledge, motivation, and task demands.
