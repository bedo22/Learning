## Key findings (one line each, with supporting quote)

- **Restraint is not one construct** — flexible vs rigid restraint are distinct; they predict different eating outcomes.
- **Rigid restraint binges; flexible control sustains** — the doc's flexible-control advice: rigid rules disinhibit (binge), flexible rules hold.

## Notes / caveats

- Eating-domain study; the doc generalizes the flexible-vs-rigid distinction to self-control broadly.
- The doc pairs it with Marlatt's abstinence violation — rigid rules make lapses catastrophic.
- **Does NOT support:** that flexible control is effortless — it's about rule style, not rule removal.
