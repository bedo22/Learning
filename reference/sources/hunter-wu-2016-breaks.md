## Key findings (one line each, with supporting quote)

- **Breaks can restore energy, but not all breaks do** — the study shows *which* break activities (preferred, resource-building) matter for recovery, not just taking a break.
- **Preferred break activities maximize recovery** — choosing something you like during a break predicts better resource recovery.

## Key quotes (with locations)

> "choosing workday break activities to maximize resource recovery" — title/abstract (the doc's "breaks restore resources" anchor)

## Notes / caveats

- Field diary study — self-reported breaks and recovery; correlational within-person.
- The doc uses it as evidence that breaks restore resources; the *mechanism* (preference → liking → recovery) is the doc's own reading, aligned with Hunter & Wu's findings.
- **Does NOT support:** that any break recovers; the study is about *which* breaks do, matching the doc's strict-list discrimination.
