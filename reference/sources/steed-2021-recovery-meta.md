## Key findings (one line each, with supporting quote)

- **Recovery is measurable** — 198 samples meta-analyzed; recovery constructs are reliably measurable (the doc's strict-list 0–10 scores are an application).
- **Activities, experiences, and states are distinct** — they intercorrelate only modestly, which is why the doc scores *experiences* (D·R·M·C) not just activities.
- **Recovery can be trained** — deliberate training programs reliably raise recovery and well-being (the doc's "recovery is a skill" implication).

## Notes / caveats

- Meta-analysis of correlational/longitudinal studies — associations, not causal proof, but the *training* programs provide intervention evidence.
- The doc cites it as 2021 (print volume year); online-first was 2019.
- **Does NOT support:** that *play specifically* recovers; Steed covers recovery broadly (detachment/relaxation/mastery/control), and the doc's play-channel claim comes from Koçak/Sonnentag, not Steed.
