## Notes / caveats

- Trait-correlational design — playfulness and coping co-vary; direction argued, not proven causally.
- The doc cites it in the playfulness cluster; the recovery mechanism remains the REQ (Sonnentag).
- **Does NOT support:** that being playful guarantees recovery — it's a trait association.
