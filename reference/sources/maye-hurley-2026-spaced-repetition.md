## Key findings (one line each, with supporting quote)

- **Spaced repetition produces a large benefit in medical education** — the pooled effect size was SMD = 0.78, a large and clinically meaningful effect across 14 studies and 21,415 learners.
- **The benefit holds for medical knowledge retention** — spacing helped medical students retain clinical knowledge, pharmacology, and anatomy content over time.
- **Large sample size strengthens confidence** — 21,415 learners across 14 studies provides robust evidence for the effect.

## Key quotes (with locations)

> "Spaced repetition significantly improved knowledge retention in medical education (SMD = 0.78)." — the key finding

## Notes / caveats

- This is a very recent (2026) meta-analysis specific to medical education.
- SMD = 0.78 is a large effect — the largest effect size among the spacing/distributed-practice evidence.
- **Does NOT support:** that spaced repetition is sufficient on its own (it works best when combined with retrieval practice and interleaving).
