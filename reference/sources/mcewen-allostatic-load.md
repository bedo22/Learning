## Key findings (one line each, with supporting quote)

- **Stress mediators protect acutely but damage chronically** — allostasis: short-term stress responses adapt; repeated/overworked responses produce allostatic load.
- **Allostatic load is cumulative wear** — the body pays for over-activation of stress systems over time.
