## Notes / caveats

- Annual Review paper — comprehensive synthesis, not a single experiment. The review covers the full landscape of workplace play research.
- The doc's distinction between "playful work" and "play as recovery" is supported by this review's framework: playful work design is an organizational intervention; play-as-recovery is an individual recovery mechanism.
- **Does NOT support:** that playful work replaces the need for off-work play, or that workplace playfulness training is equivalent to play-as-recovery.
