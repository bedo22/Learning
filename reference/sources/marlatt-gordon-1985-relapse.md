## Key findings (one line each, with supporting quote)

- **The abstinence violation effect** — after a lapse, the individual attributes it to internal failure and abandons the goal (relapse follows).
- **Rigid rules binge** — the doc's flexible-control advice: rigid all-or-nothing rules make a lapse catastrophic.

## Key quotes (with locations)
