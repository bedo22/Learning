## Key findings (one line each, with supporting quote)

- **Three basic needs: autonomy, competence, relatedness** — SDT's three psychological needs; satisfying them drives intrinsic motivation and well-being.
- **Intrinsic motivation is supported by need-satisfaction** — autonomy + competence + relatedness facilitate intrinsic motivation (SDT's core claim).

## Key quotes (with locations)

> "the needs for competence, relatedness, and autonomy" — SDT's three needs (the doc's affiliation/relatedness anchor)

## Notes / caveats

- SDT is a broad theory; the doc uses relatedness for the DRAMMA affiliation need and intrinsic motivation for play's voluntary/liking core.
- The doc's "socializing meets a need rest cannot touch" is a faithful application of relatedness.
- **Does NOT support:** that relatedness-satisfaction alone is recovery — it's one need; the doc's recovery channels are separate.
