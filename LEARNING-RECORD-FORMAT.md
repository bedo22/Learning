# Learning Record Format

Titled `0001-<dash-case-name>.md` in `learning-records/`. Like an architectural decision record: capture non-obvious lessons, decisions, and insights that may need revising later, or that drive future sessions.

## Sections
- **Date**
- **Topic**
- **Context**: Why this came up.
- **Key insight / decision**: The non-obvious thing learned or decided.
- **Evidence**: What supports it (source, experience).
- **Revises**: (optional) A previous record this updates.
- **Next**: What this implies for future sessions.

## Rules
- Increment the number each time (`0001`, `0002`, …).
- Keep it short. This is a decision log, not a lesson.
