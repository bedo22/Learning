Lesson 08 · Phase 2: Enablers

Find Your Lost 10 Hours

From Dr. Justin Sung · "Learn to Learn" — Part 4

Your schedule keeps failing, and you blame yourself. But most schedules fail for one boring reason: they're fantasy, not reality. You plan what should happen; you never check what actually does. The fix isn't more discipline — it's measurement.

Maintain a calendar, then face reality

  1. Put everything on the calendar. Work, learning, family, chores, sleep, exercise, even coffee with a friend. The reference says maintaining a calendar with blocks for everything "alone solves most time-management problems."
  2. Track what actually happens. This is the step nobody does. At day's end, write what you really did next to what you planned. The gap is your truth.
  3. Under-schedule > over-schedule. Plan to do less than you'd like. Over-scheduling = always behind, always guilty. Under-scheduling is honest and plannable.
