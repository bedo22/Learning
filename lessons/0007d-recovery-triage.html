The Recovery Triage

From the Play as Recovery reference · when you need play

Not every tired feeling needs the same fix. Play is the right recovery for one depleted system — and the wrong one for the others. Run the triage in order; the dominant signal wins:

Depleted systemSignalRecovery
Sleep / energyHeavy eyelids, yawning, sleep debtSleep (naps count)
Cognition (attention, working memory)Foggy, saturated, can't thinkRelaxation + detachment — nature, silence, no screens (see Rest & Recovery)
BodyHours sitting, stiffness, restlessnessMovement (10-min walk)
Self-regulation / controlRigid, serious, irritable at small friction, bored-but-wired, stuck, "what's the point?", monochromaticPlay

Rest is not one switch — it's a palette of needs

Play is a mode switch, not a cure-all. If the signal says cognition, play will entertain but not restore — you're loading the same cognitive systems. If the signal says sleep, even perfect play leaves you exhausted. Match the recovery to the loaded system. That's the effort-recovery model (Meijman & Mulder): recovery happens when no further demands are made on the systems that were loaded during work.

Recap: Match the recovery to the loaded system: sleep for sleep, nature for cognition, movement for the body, play for self-regulation. The dominant signal wins.

Your win: You can triage your next low point in 10 seconds — name the drained system, then apply its fix instead of reaching for your default.
