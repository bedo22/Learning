Lesson 17 · Phase 4: Encoding

Maps, Not Lists

From Dr. Justin Sung · "Learn to Learn" (Full Course, Ch. 4)

Mind mapping is a skill you progress through — not a one-off technique. It fails when you draw a map without higher-order thinking: genuinely evaluating, prioritizing, and restructuring to find the best connections. The levels make this visible.

The four levels of mapping

LevelWhat it looks likeThinking quality
0 Linear notesSentence-by-sentence transcription or summary.Almost no learning — "may as well have used ChatGPT." Brain asleep.
1 Messy nonlinearSome lines & arrows connecting obvious things; messy, overwhelming.Getting used to organizing nonlinearly; feels worse than linear notes.
2 StructuredActively deciding structure; grouping related ideas; tidier.Higher-order thinking — the OECD calls this crucial for modern learners. Where mind mapping starts working.
3 SuperchargedFully optimized, memory-boosting maps.Habitual higher-order thinking; 2–6 weeks with guidance, 2–6 months alone.

Why maps work: your brain is a network

Visual processing is 20–40,000× faster than text. Nonlinear notes that physically represent connections on paper let your brain see the networks it works in — the schema from orders of learning. If mapping didn't work for you, it's not the map — you weren't doing higher-order thinking.

Spot 0 vs spot 2 — the test

Level 0: transcribing, ordering the notes, drawing the map while thinking "that's it." Level 2: deciding the structure, grouping related ideas, asking "what's the best connection here?" If your maps aren't improving understanding, memory, or application, you're at level 0–1 — avoiding the effortful thinking that makes the map work.

Recap: Mind mapping is a skill with levels: 0 (linear, brain asleep) → 1 (messy nonlinear) → 2 (structured, higher-order — where it starts working) → 3 (supercharged, habitual). The map is the surface; the effortful thinking is the point.

Your win: You can spot your own level: next time you map something, check — are you transcribing (level 0) or deciding the structure and grouping ideas (level 2)?
